Why ReelKar Exists

Across India, lakhs of independent jewellery shops, saree boutiques, local bakeries, and bridal salons have gorgeous physical stock. But today, they are losing customers daily to venture-funded brands and social-first sellers who flood Instagram with video content.

A shopkeeper has 10 minutes between customer visits. They take photos on their phone, but hiring video editors or learning complex editing apps like CapCut, Premiere, or InShot is out of reach. Most reels go unmade, and valuable inventory sits unseen.

ReelKar is built to level the playing field. Five photos turn into an aesthetic, moving vertical reel with original sound and culturally natural captions in seconds. Zero editing timelines, zero copyright strikes, and a direct WhatsApp CTA to turn views into immediate sales.
